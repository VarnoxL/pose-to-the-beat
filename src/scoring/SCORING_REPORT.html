One move, start to finish

The move's timeline

A new move comes every 4 beats. The character shows it 3 beats early, then each player's best frame within ±150 ms of the beat counts.

The core rule

How one player is graded

Each frame's error is the weighted mean angle error over the joints the camera can see. Arms weigh 1 and legs 0.5. A joint counts only if all 3 of its landmarks have visibility of at least 0.5, and a frame counts only if at least 4 joints do.

Checks

What the test covers

Run node src/scoring/scoring.test.mjs. It needs Node 24 and no new dependencies. All of these pass:

  • Perfect, good and miss at the thresholds, and a wrong pose that doesn't count while its joint is hidden.
  • A frame with too few visible joints is ignored and grades as a miss.
  • The best frame in the window wins, and frames outside the window are ignored.
  • An absent player loses a life, and a second miss eliminates them and crowns the other player.
  • A copied trap eliminates; a trap ignored costs nothing.
  • Both players out on the same move with tied scores: the reigning champion keeps the crown.
  • Tempo climbs one tier per measure and steps down one at a time.
  • Five deck passes each deal all 10 moves with no back-to-back repeat, and the first 3 moves are never traps.
